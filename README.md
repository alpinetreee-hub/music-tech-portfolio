# Amber Shi — Music, Science & Technology Portfolio

I've studied piano for twelve years, earning ABRSM Grade 8 with Distinction and performing at Carnegie Hall. Along the way I became curious about how sound works and how it connects to our bodies and minds, which led me to build interactive music systems with Max/MSP, Jitter, and microcontrollers. My projects explore the connections between movement, the brain, emotion, and sound.

**▶ Watch my 2-minute introduction:** [Video](https://youtu.be/-Ihofp8EoaM)

# Hearing California (2025)

An original composition inspired by the sounds and sights of California, paired with 3D visuals of the state's wildlife.

**Demo video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://youtu.be/E08C860NMjw)

## Music
Composed entirely with MIDI instruments in Ableton Live, in A1–B–A2 form. A four-note motif carried by piano and strings is developed through the B section with flute and ambient pads, then returns in A2 with a hybrid bass.

## Visuals
- Built in Max/Jitter: 3D animal models blended with jit.gen and drawn as point clouds.
- A beat-synced scale animation makes the animals pulse with the music.
- Bubble-like spheres (jit.gl.multiple and jit.gl.gridshape) and smooth camera rotations, with scene changes every two measures.

# Delightful (2026)

**Composed, performed, and programmed by Amber Shi (2026)**

**▶ Watch:** [Delightful on YouTube](https://youtu.be/bWObbVEo3qg)

***Delightful* is a piano piece I composed myself in 2026**, and in this video I perform it live while Max/MSP listens to me play. As I play, a Max patch analyzes the piano's sound in real time, especially its high-frequency content, which changes with how hard and how brightly I play. It uses that information to shape the pitch and volume of synthesized bass, pad, and guitar parts that I also designed in Max/MSP. The result is a dialogue between acoustic and electronic sound: the synthesized instruments follow my phrasing, growing when I play with more energy and softening when I play gently, so no two performances sound exactly the same.
